## Cinematographers — Naturalists & Available Light
<!-- source: core -->
- Emmanuel Lubezki | Natural light obsession. The Revenant, Tree of Life, Birdman. Wide lens, long take, magic hour or nothing. Three consecutive Oscars
- Roger Deakins | Naturalistic precision, single-source devotion. Blade Runner 2049 fog, Skyfall shadows, 1917 continuity. The living standard
- Robby Müller | Natural, spare, European. Paris Texas desert, Down by Law B&W, Wenders and Jarmusch minimalism. Light as weather, not design
- Sven Nykvist | Bergman's eye. Soft, precise, northern light. Cries and Whispers red, Fanny and Alexander warmth. Two Oscars, total humility
- Néstor Almendros | Days of Heaven's magic hour, Truffaut's intimacy. Wrote the book — literally: A Man with a Camera
- Bradford Young | Low light master. Arrival muted palette, Selma intimacy, When They See Us shadow. Black visual language, darkness as warmth
- Ari Wegner | Power of the Dog precision, The Favourite wide lens, Zone of Interest's surveillance lock. Female gaze, landscape as psychology
- Raoul Coutard | Godard's cameraman, French New Wave handheld, Breathless street shooting. Invented modern documentary-fiction hybrid photography
- Haskell Wexler | Who's Afraid of Virginia Woolf B&W intensity, Medium Cool's riot footage, Bound for Glory's first Steadicam feature. Activist with a light meter
- Malik Hassan Sayeed | He Got Game's Brooklyn, Belly's neon, Lemonade's visual album. Hip-hop cinema's defining eye, Black American light
- Hoyte van Hoytema | Nolan's collaborator. Interstellar IMAX, Dunkirk grain, Oppenheimer B&W IMAX, Her's soft digital. Technical precision serving emotion

- Caravaggio | Extreme chiaroscuro, spotlight from darkness, biblical drama, dirty realism. Judith Beheading Holofernes, Calling of St Matthew
- Rembrandt van Rijn | Self-portrait master, warm light in darkness, triangle cheek light. The Night Watch, The Jewish Bride, Bathsheba
- Johannes Vermeer | Window light perfection, domestic intimacy, Dutch golden age. Girl with a Pearl Earring, The Milkmaid
- Diego Velázquez | Las Meninas painting-about-painting, royal portraiture with psychological depth, grey-silver palette. Pope Innocent X
- Artemisia Gentileschi | Judith Slaying Holofernes female violence, Caravaggio's techniques with a woman's fury, baroque's most visceral storyteller
- El Greco | Elongated mystical figures, Toledo storm-light, spiritual ecstasy in distorted form, proto-Expressionism 300 years early
- Hieronymus Bosch | Garden of Earthly Delights teeming surrealism, medieval nightmare logic, hell as populated landscape
- Pieter Bruegel the Elder | Hunters in the Snow, peasant life as epic, aerial village perspective, the landscape teeming with human comedy
- J.M.W. Turner | Light dissolving form, proto-Impressionism, maritime drama. Rain Steam and Speed, The Fighting Temeraire
- Francisco Goya | Saturn Devouring His Son, Third of May, Black Paintings. The bridge from classical to modern, beauty to horror
- Caspar David Friedrich | Wanderer Above the Sea of Fog, Romantic sublime, human figure before vastness, German spiritual landscape
- Jean-Auguste-Dominique Ingres | La Grande Odalisque impossible spine, classical perfection pushed to fetishistic extremes. Line as religion

- Claude Monet | Light as subject, series painting, water lilies, haystacks. Rouen Cathedral in twenty lights, plein air, the eye retrained

- Paul Cézanne | Mont Sainte-Victoire, the apple, geometric structure beneath Impressionist color. Father of Cubism
- Vincent van Gogh | Starry Night turbulence, impasto energy, color as psychological state. Irises, Wheatfield with Crows, the bedroom
- Paul Gauguin | Tahitian color, spiritual primitivism, flat decorative planes. Where Do We Come From? What Are We? Where Are We Going?
- Georges Seurat | Pointillist precision, La Grande Jatte frozen Sunday, color theory as practice, every dot a decision
- Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec | Moulin Rouge cabaret, the poster as art, bohemian night life, the lithographic line
- Gustav Klimt | Gold leaf and pattern, The Kiss, decorative-figurative fusion, Viennese Secession. Adele Bloch-Bauer's golden cage
- Egon Schiele | Angular bodies, sexual confrontation, raw line, self-portrait as provocation. Klimt's student stripped of gold, only nerve

## Painters — Modernists: Expressionism through Surrealism
<!-- source: core -->
- Edvard Munch | The Scream, anxiety as landscape, vibrating color. The Sick Child, Puberty, Madonna, proto-Expressionist psychological self-portraiture
- Wassily Kandinsky | First abstract paintings, Composition series, synesthesia, Bauhaus theory. The spiritual in art made literal
- Piet Mondrian | Broadway Boogie Woogie, primary colors and black grid, De Stijl purity. Tree series: figuration dissolving into abstraction
- Kazimir Malevich | Black Square on white ground. Suprematism. The most radical painting ever — nothing as everything
- Marcel Duchamp | Nude Descending a Staircase, Fountain. Anti-retinal, anti-painting, the idea as art. Changed everything by refusing
- Salvador Dalí | Persistence of Memory, paranoiac-critical method, hyperreal dreamscape, melting logic, self-promotion as medium
- René Magritte | Treachery of Images, bowler-hat men, everyday surrealism. Son of Man, Golconda, philosophy as painting
- Max Ernst | Frottage, grattage, collage novel. Surrealist texture, mechanical and organic merging, forest and eye
- Giorgio de Chirico | Metaphysical painting, deserted piazzas, elongated shadows, mannequins. The eerie afternoon, uncanny architecture
- Frida Kahlo | Self-portrait as autobiography, pain as color, Mexican identity, broken body decorated, surrealist who refused the label
- Marc Chagall | Floating lovers, village memory, stained-glass color, Jewish folk imagery. Paris and Vitebsk merged in dream

## Painters — Abstract Expressionists & Color Field
<!-- source: core -->
- Jackson Pollock | Drip paintings, action painting, the arena. Autumn Rhythm, Number 1A. Painting as event, mythic American painter
- Mark Rothko | Color field meditation, Seagram Murals, the chapel, floating rectangles that make people cry. Scale as spiritual instrument
- Willem de Kooning | Woman series, aggressive figuration in abstract field, slashing brushwork. Excavation, the body never fully gone
- Franz Kline | Black and white monumental gesture, bridge-girder brushstrokes. Mahoning, Painting Number 2, cityscape as abstraction
- Helen Frankenthaler | Soak-stain technique, Mountains and Sea, poured color on raw canvas. Bridge between Pollock and Color Field
- Agnes Martin | Grid paintings, trembling lines, quiet meditation, pale color, Zen minimalism. Maximum restraint, maximum feeling
- Barnett Newman | Zip paintings, Who's Afraid of Red Yellow and Blue. The vertical line dividing color as metaphysical event
- Robert Motherwell | Elegy to the Spanish Republic series, B&W monumentality, intellectual painter, the shape of grief
- Joan Mitchell | Sunflower paintings, gestural color, landscape-into-abstraction, Giverny studio. Most underrated AbEx painter, ferocious

- David Hockney | A Bigger Splash, pool paintings, joiners, iPad art. Yorkshire to LA, seeing itself as subject
- Ed Ruscha | Standard Station, Every Building on the Sunset Strip. LA as text, the word as image, deadpan landscape
- Yayoi Kusama | Infinity rooms, dot obsession, obliteration, cosmic self-dissolution. Net paintings, pumpkins, self consumed by repetition
