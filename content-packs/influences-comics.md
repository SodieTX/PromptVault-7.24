# Pack: Manga, Comics & Sequential Art

## Manga Artists — Action & Shonen
- Kentaro Miura | Berserk. Crosshatch density that rivals Dürer, impossible armor design, dark fantasy at its most obsessive. Died mid-masterpiece
- Takehiko Inoue | Slam Dunk, Vagabond. Brush ink mastery, Musashi's expression worth 10,000 words. Sports manga elevated to fine art
- Eiichiro Oda | One Piece. Exaggerated proportion, kinetic action, 1000+ chapters of escalating world-building density
- Masashi Kishimoto | Naruto. Dynamic action choreography, ninja-meets-streetwear character design, emotional page layouts
- Tite Kubo | Bleach. Fashion-forward character design, negative space as weapon, coolest silhouettes in manga

## Manga Artists — Horror & Psychological
- Junji Ito | Uzumaki, Tomie, The Enigma of Amigara Fault. Body horror through obsessive pattern, the beautiful becoming grotesque
- Suehiro Maruo | Shojo Tsubaki, Ultra-Gash Inferno. Ero-guro, Taisho-era decadence, beauty and atrocity on the same page
- Shintaro Kago | Abstraction, metamorphosis, guro-comedy. Bodies as architecture, flesh as material, the absurd made anatomical

## Western Comics — Visionaries
- Moebius (Jean Giraud) | The Incal, Arzach. Clean ligne claire for sci-fi landscapes, infinite desert horizons, organic technology. Influenced Alien, Blade Runner, Akira
- Frank Miller | Sin City, Dark Knight Returns. High-contrast B&W, negative space as narrative, noir as a blunt instrument
- Bill Sienkiewicz | Elektra: Assassin, New Mutants. Mixed media collage meets superhero art, painted expressionism in a comics grid
- Dave McKean | Sandman covers, Arkham Asylum. Photo-collage, paint, digital compositing. Comics as gallery installation
- Mike Mignola | Hellboy. Angular shadow, Expressionist blocks, Lovecraft meets folk art. Less is more applied to monsters

## Western Comics — Contemporary
- Fiona Staples | Saga. Painterly digital, diverse character design, sci-fi warmth, the anti-grimdark
- Tradd Moore | Silver Surfer Black, Luther Strode. Psychedelic line density, Kirby energy on acid, bodies as kinetic sculpture
- James Stokoe | Orc Stain, Godzilla. Obsessive detail, every surface has texture, maximalist worldbuilding in ink
- Tillie Walden | Spinning, On a Sunbeam. Quiet color, vast negative space, queer coming-of-age in cosmic architecture
